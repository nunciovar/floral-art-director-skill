# Example — Starry Night, full v0.2 pipeline

A complete **Floral Design Presentation Package** for Vincent van Gogh's *The Starry Night*: the reference is translated through design language into a professional, manufacturable floral artwork, delivered as the LEVEL 1 Floral Design Board plus the LEVEL 2 Final Bouquet Visualization.

> **Principle applied:** the bouquet must first exist as a real floral artwork, and then resemble the reference image.

## Reference Image

Vincent van Gogh, *The Starry Night* (1889). A swirling cobalt-and-indigo night sky with broad painterly brush strokes, a glowing cadmium-yellow crescent moon and star halos, teal-cyan undertones, and a dark rolling village silhouette at the bottom. The colors are deliberately non-realistic; the true subject is **motion and inner light**.

# LEVEL 1 — Floral Design Board

## 1. Reference Analysis

- **Color structure** — main: cobalt and indigo blue; supporting: teal-cyan and midnight blue; accent: bright cadmium yellow (moon, stars)
- **Brightness** — dark field punctuated by small luminous yellow points
- **Saturation** — high for blue, intense for yellow — deliberately saturated
- **Warm/cool balance** — cool field, warm glowing counterpoint
- **Light and shadow** — glowing halos, no single source; inner light
- **Texture** — thick painterly brush strokes, heavy impasto
- **Composition** — swirling, dynamic, high energy, sky dominates
- **Emotional atmosphere** — expressive, emotional, cosmic, a little feverish

## 2. Style DNA

```text
Style DNA:
```


## 4. Floral Design Concept

**Name:** The Swirling Sky / 星空漩涡

- **Design philosophy** — build the bouquet like a painting: the night is a deep blue field of real flowers, the moon is a concentrated yellow cluster, the stars are scattered yellow accents, and the swirl — the painting's true subject — becomes curved line material and a spiral arrangement. The painterly quality lives in arrangement and line, never in impossible flower colors.
- **Bouquet structure** — medium-to-large expressive bouquet, swirling asymmetrical silhouette, medium-dense core opening out at the edges, ~36 stems.
- **Emotional expression** — cosmic, expressive, luminous yet grounded.

## 5. Floral Reality Check

- **Color Feasibility**
  - Cobalt / indigo night: **natural approximation** — deep-blue iris, blue delphinium, blue hydrangea, cornflower. True painterly cobalt does not exist as a flower color; the darkest blue weight is reinforced by deep navy wrapping rather than by dyed flowers.
  - Cadmium yellow: **natural approximation** — bright-yellow ranunculus, lemon-yellow spray roses, yellow tulips. If a truer cadmium moon is essential, the yellow cluster may use a single `dyed flower` — but the default is all-natural.
  - Teal undertones: **material** — one layer of teal organza, not a flower.
- **Material Translation**
  - Swirl / brush strokes → curly-willow line material + spiral binding in `composition`, not painted petals.
  - Moon glow and star halos → concentrated yellow focal cluster + scattered small yellow accents under directional `lighting`, with a fine gold-wire accent for starlight (`accessory`).
  - Painterly texture → layered foliage and varied leaf forms; the render brief supplies the painterly light.
- **Seasonal Availability** — iris (spring) and cornflower (summer) are seasonal; substitutes supplied below (iris ↔ purple gladiolus; cornflower ↔ grape hyacinth). Yellow ranunculus is winter-spring; substitute yellow tulips or spray roses.
- **Construction Feasibility** — medium-to-large spiral-built bouquet, ~36 stems; curly willow provides the outer swirl frame and is anchored into the binding point, not floating; stable and buildable by a working florist.

## 7. Structural Sketch

- **Height hierarchy** — delphinium and curly willow stand highest (the swirl frame); iris next; hydrangea fills the core body; cornflower dots the surface as star points; eucalyptus closes the base.
- **Focal flower** — the concentrated yellow ranunculus cluster, positioned high-center as the moon.
- **Supporting flowers** — hydrangea and iris carry the blue night field around the moon.
- **Line flowers** — curly willow and delphinium carry the spiral motion outward.
- **Wrapping** — deep navy paper backing with a teal organza overlay, gathered at the binding point; thin solar-yellow ribbon; gold wire at the binding.

## 8. Alternative Views

- **Front view** — full field: moon cluster at high center, swirling willow frame, navy + teal wrap.
- **Side view** — spiral depth: the yellow cluster reads as the forward energy point; line material arcs backward.
- **Detail view** — close-up of the moon cluster (yellow ranunculus against hydrangea) and the binding point with gold wire and ribbon.

# Example 04 — Amber Within

## Reference Image

A high-contrast monochrome close-up photograph of an eye, with a single small but visually dominant amber-orange reflection inside the pupil. Everything else is black and charcoal: fine directional eyelash lines, minimal grey transitions, a deep dark field. The composition is centered and intimate; the emotion is restrained, cinematic, quietly tense. The design challenge is not to reproduce the eye literally, but to translate its focal-color hierarchy, line structure, material qualities, and restrained emotional atmosphere into floristry.

## ↓ Style DNA

**Visual analysis**
- Primary: black and charcoal (monochrome dark field)
- Secondary: warm amber-orange (the single focal point)
- Accent: small white specular highlights
- Brightness: very dark field with one small luminous amber point
- Saturation: low and monochrome overall; the amber focal is saturated
- Temperature: cool dark field, warm glowing counterpoint
- Light: a single specular amber reflection — no natural light source
- Composition: close-up, centered pupil, fine radiating eyelash lines
- Mood: noir, intimate, silent, with a restrained inner tension

**Keywords**
`Noir Intimacy · Amber Glow · Silent Tension · Minimal Contrast · Hidden Emotion · Cinematic Darkness`

## ↓ Floral Design Concept

**Name:** Amber Within / 琥珀之心

An asymmetric, cinematic bouquet built around a concentrated amber focal point: warmth hidden inside darkness. Rather than copying the eye's shape, the design uses focal hierarchy, negative space, fine linear branch movement, and a controlled warm color transition to reproduce the emotional structure of the reference.

- **Style:** noir, cinematic, contemporary
- **Size:** medium
- **Silhouette:** asymmetric, with fine line material radiating outward and up
- **Density:** concentrated at the amber focal core, open in the dark surround
- **Design logic:** orange tulips = the amber reflection (concentrated, never flooding the bouquet); champagne roses = the warm light fading into the dark; white lisianthus + small white filler flowers = the specular highlights; curly willow and dark fern = the eyelash / contour lines; dark matte wrapping, dark foliage, and negative space = the monochrome darkness — never fabricated black flowers

## ↓ Floral Reality Check

- **Color Feasibility**
  - Amber orange: **natural flower color** — orange tulips.
  - Champagne: **natural flower color** — champagne / nude-tone roses.
  - White: **natural flower color** — white lisianthus and small white filler flowers.
  - Black / charcoal: **material** — dark wrapping, dark foliage and branches, plus negative space, not fabricated black flowers.
- **Material Translation**
  - Monochrome darkness → dark matte wrapping + dark foliage + negative space in `composition`.
  - The amber reflection / specular light → small white flowers as restrained highlight points; a translucent smoke-black film carries the reflective surface quality.
  - Eyelash and contour lines → curly willow and fine branches as radiating line material.
- **Seasonal Availability** — orange tulips are strongly seasonal; substitutes: orange lisianthus, orange ranunculus, orange garden rose.
- **Construction Feasibility** — realistic asymmetric hand-tied bouquet; line materials extend above and beyond the central floral mass while the focal tulips stay visually concentrated; stable and buildable by a working florist.

## ↓ Image Generation Brief

Generate a premium florist-portfolio still-life photograph of an asymmetric, cinematic bouquet built around a single concentrated amber focal point. Real orange tulips form the warm focal cluster; champagne roses carry the amber warmth out into the dark surround; white lisianthus and small white filler flowers add restrained highlight points; curly willow and dark fern supply fine radiating line movement. Wrap in dark matte paper with one translucent smoke-black layer and a deep black ribbon. Dark, low-key background with soft directional light that reads as a single amber glow. The bouquet must remain physically plausible, asymmetric, restrained, cinematic, and amber-focused — avoid a fantasy bouquet or an over-decorated black-and-gold arrangement.

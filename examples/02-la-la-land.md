# Example 02 — La La Land

## Reference Image

A cinematic still in the La La Land palette: a deep violet-to-blue night sky, silhouetted palm fronds, and warm amber-gold city lights scattered as small glowing dots across the frame. Strong cool-warm contrast between the dark blue dusk and the golden glow, gentle film grain, soft halation, dreamy romantic mood.

## ↓ Style DNA

**Visual analysis**
- Primary: violet and deep blue night
- Secondary: dusky lavender, plum, charcoal blue
- Accent: warm amber-gold (the city lights)
- Brightness: low overall, with small bright glowing points
- Saturation: deep but muted in the field, saturated gold accent
- Temperature: predominantly cool, counterpoint of warm light
- Light: scattered point sources, soft glow, halation
- Composition: dark field with distributed small warm anchors
- Mood: romantic, cinematic, nostalgic, a little melancholic

**Keywords**
`Violet · Midnight · Cinematic · Starstruck · Romantic · Glow`

## ↓ Floral Design Concept

**Name:** Dusk in the City / 午夜星光

A medium-to-large, lush-but-not-heavy romantic bouquet. Deep purple and blue create the night field; warm amber and gold appear only as small focal accents, echoing the scattered city lights rather than flooding the frame. The cool-warm tension — the entire emotional core of the reference — is carried by real flower colors and restrained metallic material.

- **Style:** romantic, cinematic, evening
- **Size:** medium-to-large
- **Silhouette:** rounded but airy, with lifted line material
- **Density:** medium, layered, with breathing room
- **Design logic:** violet/blue mass = the dusk field; amber focal spray = the glowing lights; a touch of gold ribbon and wire = halation and starlight; dark foliage = the silhouetted palms

## ↓ Image Generation Brief

Generate a premium florist-portfolio still-life photograph of a medium-to-large romantic evening bouquet in a violet-and-midnight-blue palette with small warm amber-gold focal accents. Feature realistic dark-purple lisianthus, blue delphinium, deep-blue hydrangea, iris, pale lilac, and eucalyptus, with a few warm apricot-gold spray roses as the only warm focal points. Wrap in deep navy matte paper with a single fine gold ribbon and a restrained gold-wire detail. Dark, low-key background with soft directional light that reads as scattered city lights; gentle film grain and halation; crisp botanical detail. The bouquet must remain physically plausible and commercially buildable. Avoid heavy metallic decoration and avoid strong fluorescent blues.
